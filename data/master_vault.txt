Section 103 BNS (Purana Section 302 IPC) - Murder (Hatya / Katl):
Murder ya hatya par pehle Section 302 IPC lagta tha, jo ab naye kanoon me Section 103(1) BNS (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023) hai. Saza: Maut (death penalty) ya ajeevan karawas (life imprisonment) aur fine. Mob lynching (5 ya zyada log) par Section 103(2) BNS ke tehat maut ya ajeevan karawas hota hai.

Section 109 BNS (Purana Section 307 IPC) - Attempt to Murder (Hatya Ka Prayas / Jaanleva Hamla):
Jaanleva hamla karne par pehle Section 307 IPC tha, ab Section 109 BNS hai. Saza: 10 saal tak ki jail aur fine. Gambhir chot aane par ajeevan karawas ya 10 saal tak ki jail.

Section 115 & 117 BNS (Purana Section 323 & 325 IPC) - Hurt and Grievous Hurt (Maarpeet / Chot / Physical Assault):
Simple maarpeet par pehle Section 323 IPC tha, ab Section 115(2) BNS hai (1 saal tak jail ya Rs 10,000 fine). Haddi todna ya gambhir chot par pehle Section 325 IPC tha, ab Section 117 BNS hai (7 saal tak jail aur fine). Dangerous weapon use karne par Section 118 BNS (10 saal tak jail).

Section 137 & 140 BNS (Purana Section 363 & 364A IPC) - Kidnapping and Abduction (Apaharan / Firoti):
Apaharan par pehle Section 363 IPC tha, ab Section 137 BNS hai (7 saal tak jail). Firoti (ransom) ke liye kidnapping par pehle Section 364A IPC tha, ab Section 140 BNS hai (maut ki saza ya ajeevan karawas).

Section 351 & 352 BNS (Purana Section 503 & 506 IPC) - Criminal Intimidation (Dhamki Dena / Jaan Se Marne Ki Dhamki):
Dhamki dene par pehle Section 506 IPC tha, ab Section 351 aur 352 BNS hai. Normal dhamki par 2 saal tak jail. Jaan se marne ki dhamki par 7 saal tak ki jail aur fine.

Section 303 BNS (Purana Section 379 IPC) - Theft (Chori / Pocket Maarna / Phone Chori):
Chori par pehle Section 379 IPC tha, ab Section 303(2) BNS hai. Saza: 3 saal tak jail, fine ya dono. Pehli baar minor chori par community service ka option hai.

Section 309 & 310 BNS (Purana Section 392 & 395 IPC) - Robbery and Dacoity (Loot aur Dakaiti):
Loot (robbery) par pehle Section 392 IPC tha, ab Section 309 BNS hai (10 saal tak jail). Dakaiti (5 ya zyada log) par pehle Section 395 IPC tha, ab Section 310 BNS hai (ajeevan karawas ya 10 saal tak jail).

Section 318 BNS (Purana Section 420 & 417 IPC) - Cheating and Fraud (Dhokhadhadi / Farziwada / Online Scam):
Dhokhadhadi par pehle Section 420 IPC tha, ab Section 318(4) BNS hai (7 saal tak jail aur fine). Simple cheating par Section 318(2) BNS me 3 saal tak jail hai.

Section 63, 64 & 70 BNS (Purana Section 375, 376 & 376D IPC) - Rape and Gang Rape (Balatkar / Sexual Assault):
Rape par pehle Section 376 IPC tha, ab Section 64(1) BNS hai (kam se kam 10 saal se lekar ajeevan karawas). Gang rape par pehle Section 376D IPC tha, ab Section 70 BNS hai (kam se kam 20 saal ya ajeevan karawas). Mahila ka bayaan aur FIR mahila police officer darj karegi (Section 173 BNSS).

Section 85 & 86 BNS (Purana Section 498A IPC) - Domestic Violence (Gharelu Hinsa / Dahej Utpeedan):
Pati ya sasural walon dwara atyachar aur dahej par pehle Section 498A IPC tha, ab Section 85 BNS hai (3 saal tak jail aur fine). Helpline: 1091 ya 112.

Section 106 BNS (Purana Section 304A IPC) & Section 134A MVA - Road Accident & Hit and Run (Takkar Maarna):
Laparwahi se gaadi chala kar maut par pehle Section 304A IPC tha, ab Section 106(1) BNS hai (5 saal tak jail). Takkar maar kar bhagne par (Hit and Run) Section 106(2) BNS ke tehat 10 saal tak ki jail aur fine hai. Section 134A Motor Vehicles Act ke tehat madad karne wale Good Samaritan ko police ya hospital pareshan nahi kar sakti. Emergency: 112 / 108.

Section 185 Motor Vehicles Act - Drunk and Drive (Nashe Me Driving):
Sharab ya nashe me gaadi chalane par Section 185 MVA ke tehat pehli baar me 6 mahine jail ya Rs 10,000 fine. Dusri baar me 2 saal jail aur Rs 15,000 fine. Police gaadi impound kar sakti hai.

Driving Documents - Documents Required While Driving a Vehicle:
While driving a private vehicle in India, the driver should carry a valid Driving Licence, Registration Certificate (RC), motor insurance certificate or policy, and a valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate. Valid electronic versions in DigiLocker or mParivahan may be shown when they are available for verification. Commercial vehicles may additionally require a permit, fitness certificate, and applicable tax documents. Requirements can vary by vehicle type and current state/RTO rules.

Section 179 BNSS (Purana Section 160 CrPC) - Police Summons (Phone Par Bulana / Notice):
Police kisi bhi nagarik ko phone ya maukhik (verbal) taur par thaaney nahi bula sakti. Likhit notice (Section 179 BNSS) zaroori hai. 15 saal se kam umra, 60 saal se upar ke log, mahila aur divyang ko thaaney nahi bulaya ja sakta; puchtach unke ghar par hogi.

Section 35, 37 & 43 BNSS (Purana Section 41 & 46 CrPC) - Arrest Rules and Night Arrest of Women:
7 saal se kam saza wale apradh me police direct arrest nahi kar sakti, Section 35(3) BNSS ka Notice of Appearance dena padega. Mahila ko sooryast ke baad aur sooryoday se pehle girftar nahi kiya ja sakta, sirf mahila police dwara Magistrate ki permission se hi arrest hoga.

Lost Property (LDR) vs FIR - Mobile Gum Hona ya Kho Jana:
Mobile phone ya documents sirf kho jane par FIR darj nahi hoti kyunki saman khona apradh nahi hai. Iske liye State Police portal se online Lost Document Report (LDR) ya General Diary (GD) darj hoti hai. Chori hone par Section 173 BNSS ke tehat FIR hoti hai.

Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 - Online Financial Fraud:
Online banking ya UPI fraud hone par turant 1930 dial karein taaki paise freeze ho sakein, aur cybercrime.gov.in par report karein. Dhokhadhadi Section 318 BNS aur Section 66D IT Act me aati hai.

Section 173 BNSS - FIR, Zero FIR and Refusal Remedy:
Cognizable offence ki information police station me orally ya electronically di ja sakti hai. Oral information ko writing me record karke informant ko read over karna aur free copy dena hota hai. Zero FIR kisi bhi police station me lodge ho sakti hai, jurisdiction ke naam par refuse nahi ki ja sakti. Refusal par written complaint Superintendent of Police ko bheji ja sakti hai; further remedy Magistrate ke paas available ho sakti hai.

Section 47 and 48 BNSS - Arrest Grounds and Family Intimation:
Arrest karte samay police ko arrested person ko grounds of arrest batane hote hain. Bailable offence me bail right ke baare me inform karna hota hai. Police ko nominated relative, friend ya family member ko arrest aur custody location ki information deni hoti hai.

Section 58 BNSS - Magistrate Production Within 24 Hours:
Arrested person ko journey time ko chhodkar 24 hours se zyada police custody me nahi rakha ja sakta. Person ko nearest Magistrate ke saamne produce karna hota hai, subject to applicable legal exceptions and court orders.

Section 38 and 53 BNSS - Advocate and Medical Examination:
Arrested person ko interrogation ke dauran advocate se milne aur consult karne ka right hota hai, law ke limits ke subject me. Arrest ke baad registered medical practitioner se medical examination ka request kiya ja sakta hai, especially injuries document karne ke liye.

Section 185 BNSS and Section 103 BNSS - Search and Seizure Safeguards:
Search without warrant tabhi ki ja sakti hai jab law ke conditions satisfy hon aur officer grounds ko writing me record kare. Search aur seizure me applicable witness, seizure-list, copy to occupant, Magistrate reporting aur audio-video recording requirements follow karni hoti hain.

Bailable, Non-Bailable and Anticipatory Bail:
Bailable offence me bail statutory right ho sakti hai, subject to bond and applicable conditions. Non-bailable offence me bail court discretion par depend karti hai. Arrest ka apprehension ho to applicable case me Sessions Court ya High Court se anticipatory bail seek ki ja sakti hai.

Police Misconduct and Custodial Safety:
Police abuse, unlawful violence aur custodial torture ke against citizen written complaint Superintendent of Police, State Police Complaints Authority ya State Human Rights Commission ko de sakta hai. Immediate danger me 112 call karein. Exact remedy facts, medical evidence aur applicable law par depend karegi.

Cyber Crime Reporting - 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in:
Online financial fraud, UPI scam, phishing, identity theft ya unauthorized debit par turant 1930 call karein aur cybercrime.gov.in par complaint file karein. Transaction details, screenshots, phone numbers, UTR and messages preserve karein. Applicable offences facts ke basis par BNS aur Information Technology Act ke under ho sakte hain.

National Emergency Helpline 112:
Immediate threat to life, police intervention, road accident ya urgent public safety situation me 112 dial karein. Medical assistance ke liye local emergency services bhi contact karein. TrustLink emergency response ka substitute nahi hai.
